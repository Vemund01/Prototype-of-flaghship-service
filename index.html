Public Funding Navigator

A working prototype of the flagship service. A founder describes the start-up in plain language; AI extracts the facts, explicit rules check eligibility against a table of funding opportunities, AI drafts a ranked shortlist, and an advisor approves it before it is sent.

Rule check

        
Explicit rules compare the confirmed facts with each scheme's documented conditions. The AI takes no part in this step. "Needs checking" means a fact is missing or a condition needs human judgement.

        

        

        

        
Schemes with "Include in ranking" set to no (for example thin or supporting schemes) are shown here but are not ranked.

        

        

      


      

        
Draft shortlist

        
The AI ranked only schemes that passed or partly passed the rule check and wrote the reasons. Amounts, deadlines, dates checked and links are filled in from the database, not by the AI.

Limits
          
    
            
  • The rules come from the 'Rule:' columns of the database file. The built-in rules have not yet been verified against the official pages.
